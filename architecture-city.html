03 / three things to remember

The city’s simple rules

01 / FRONT DOOR

Vercel serves the storefront

Vercel is the future place where the Next.js website can live online. It does not replace Supabase’s database or identity office.

02 / CITY RECORDS

Supabase holds the important state

Accounts, listings, images, conversations, and offers live in Supabase services, not inside the browser.

03 / SECURITY GATES

RLS makes the final decision

The interface can hide a button, but database Row Level Security is the real guard that decides who may read or change each record.
